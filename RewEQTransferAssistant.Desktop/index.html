Why use RewDspAssistant.Desktop?

Lightning Fast

No more manually typing Frequency, Q, and Gain values. Transfer up to 30 bands instantly via API or Clipboard.

Zero Errors

Eliminate human error when transferring complex tunings. Ensure your DSP matches your REW calculations perfectly.

Multi-Platform DSPs

Designed to bridge the gap for popular automotive and home DSP platforms. Open architecture for profiles.

Advanced Filter Logic

We don't just copy numbers blindly. RewDspAssistant.Desktop translates REW's raw mathematical data into precise Q factors tailored to the DSP environment:

  • High Shelf (HS) & Low Shelf (LS): The Q is dynamically calculated based on the REW default Slope (S=0.9) and the exported Gain, guaranteeing an exact curve match.
  • High Pass (HP) & Low Pass (LP): The software forces the mathematically correct Q value based on your selected Crossover Alignment (e.g., Butterworth = 0.7071, Linkwitz-Riley = 0.50, Bessel = 0.5774).
  • Peak Filters (PK): Q and Gain parameters are perfectly retained and formatted according to your chosen DSP Profile.
