Advanced Filter Logic

We don't just copy numbers blindly. RewEQTransferAssistant translates REW's raw mathematical data into precise Q factors tailored to the DSP environment:

  • High Shelf (HS) & Low Shelf (LS): The Q is dynamically calculated based on the REW default Slope (S=0.9) and the exported Gain, guaranteeing an exact curve match.
  • High Pass (HP) & Low Pass (LP): The software forces the mathematically correct Q value based on your selected Crossover Alignment (e.g., Butterworth = 0.7071, Linkwitz-Riley = 0.50).
  • Peak Filters (PK): Q and Gain parameters are perfectly retained and formatted according to your chosen DSP Profile.
